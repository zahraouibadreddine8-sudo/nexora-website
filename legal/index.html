Legal Notice

Key information about who this website is for, how commercial relationships are formed, and what Nexora does—and does not—promise through the public site.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Current business statusNexora is the brand name used for this creator-partnership website and operates from Morocco. This website is currently an enquiry and information site: it does not take payment, execute sponsorship contracts, or create a paid campaign through an online checkout.

1. Website operator and commercial identity

The website is operated under the Nexora brand from Morocco. Nexora does not claim on this website to be an LLC, corporation, or other registered legal entity unless and until that is legally true and the website is updated accordingly.

For any paid campaign, the actual legal contracting and payee identity, billing details, and any legally required registration or tax information will be stated in the relevant written campaign agreement, order confirmation, vendor-onboarding documentation, or invoice before a binding commercial commitment is made.

2. Purpose of the website

The site introduces Nexora's creator-partnership services, lets brands and creators start a conversation, and provides information about Nexora's process. It is not an online marketplace, payment portal, employment portal, or self-service sponsorship-booking platform.

3. No contract from browsing or submitting a form

Visiting the site, submitting an enquiry, receiving a reply, being researched, or being considered for a campaign does not by itself create a sponsorship, representation agreement, agency relationship, employment relationship, exclusivity obligation, payment obligation, or other binding commercial commitment.

A paid campaign becomes binding only when the relevant parties agree the material commercial terms in separate written documentation.

4. Commercial terms are confirmed separately

Paid campaigns can differ in creator, deliverable, fee, payment timing, revisions, usage rights, exclusivity, cancellation terms, deadlines, approvals, shipping, disclosure requirements, and other conditions. Those terms are confirmed for the specific campaign and are not created by the general wording of this website.

5. No performance guarantee

Creator statistics, audience information, past views, engagement figures, examples, estimates, or campaign discussions do not guarantee future views, clicks, sales, conversions, revenue, or other performance unless a specific written campaign agreement expressly states otherwise.

6. Advertising and endorsement standards

Nexora expects sponsored creator content to comply with applicable advertising, consumer-protection, platform, and endorsement-disclosure requirements. Nexora does not require creators to conceal a material sponsorship or make claims they know are false. Brands remain responsible for providing accurate product information and substantiation for claims they ask creators to communicate.

7. Creator independence

Unless a separate written agreement expressly says otherwise, a creator who is discussed, researched, introduced, or included in a potential campaign is not automatically an employee, exclusive representative, or permanently represented creator of Nexora. Availability, rates, rights, and campaign acceptance must be confirmed for the relevant opportunity.

8. Website content and intellectual property

Nexora's original website copy, visual design, graphics, and branding are protected by applicable intellectual-property rules. Third-party names, trademarks, creator/channel names, platform names, images, and other third-party materials remain the property of their respective owners. Reference to a brand or creator does not by itself imply endorsement, employment, ownership, or an active commercial relationship.

9. Third-party services

The site uses third-party infrastructure to operate. Current public-site services include Vercel for hosting and Web Analytics and Web3Forms for enquiry processing. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. More detail about personal-data processing is available in the Privacy Policy.

10. Contact and privacy requests

For general questions, campaign enquiries, legal notices, or privacy requests, use Nexora's general contact form. For a privacy request, begin the message with “Privacy request.” Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, health information, authentication secrets, or unpublished trade secrets through the public form.

11. Complaints and disputes

If you believe information on the site is inaccurate, your rights have been affected, or there is a problem with a Nexora interaction, contact Nexora first with enough detail to identify the issue. Campaign-specific disputes are handled under the applicable written campaign agreement and governing law. Nothing on this site removes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

12. Privacy and data protection

Personal information submitted through the site is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. Website legal notices do not replace any CNDP notification, authorization, international-transfer, accounting, registration, or other real-world compliance step that may apply to the operator.

13. Governing framework

This public website is operated from Morocco. Website use and Nexora's public-site conduct are therefore subject to applicable Moroccan law, while mandatory laws in another jurisdiction may also apply depending on the facts of a particular user, campaign, consumer relationship, or commercial transaction.

14. Changes

Nexora may update this Legal Notice when the site's services, provider stack, business structure, or legal setup changes. The date at the top identifies the current version.

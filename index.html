Nexora frequently asked questions

01What does Nexora do?

Nexora helps match marketing needs with the right people and partners. Depending on the goal, that could mean creators, specialists, agencies, brands, or other relevant partners. We research the fit, qualify the opportunity, clarify commercial details, and help coordinate the collaboration.

02Do you work with every industry?

We are not limited to one niche. Nexora can evaluate opportunities across industries, provided the campaign, audience, channel, and creator or marketing approach make sense together.

03How do you choose people or partners for a campaign?

We look beyond a name or follower count. Depending on the campaign, we review relevance, recent work or performance, audience or market fit, commercial terms, availability, conflicts, and whether the collaboration makes practical sense.

04Does a brand need a fixed budget before contacting Nexora?

No. A budget range is useful because creator pricing can vary significantly, but you can still start with the campaign goal, audience, timing, and deliverables. We can use that context to shape the next conversation.

05How quickly does Nexora reply?

We usually reply within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday. We may occasionally reply on weekends, but weekend responses are not guaranteed. Complex enquiries, holidays, or periods of unusually high volume can take longer, so this is our normal response target rather than a guaranteed deadline.

06Does submitting an enquiry create a commitment?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation only. It does not create a sponsorship, representation agreement, exclusivity obligation, payment obligation, or binding campaign. Material campaign terms are confirmed separately in writing.

07How are payments and Nexora’s fee handled?

The payment flow, payee, creator payment timing, and any Nexora fee or commission are confirmed for the specific campaign before anyone commits. Nexora does not currently take campaign payments through the public website.

08What about usage rights, revisions, and deadlines?

Those details are campaign-specific. Deliverables, approval flow, revisions, usage or ad rights, exclusivity, deadlines, cancellation terms, and other commercial conditions should be agreed before the campaign is finalized.

09Can creators introduce themselves to Nexora?

Yes. Creators can submit their channel or profile, niche, audience information, recent performance, and rates if available. Introducing yourself does not guarantee a paid campaign, but it gives Nexora context for relevant future opportunities.

10Does Nexora guarantee campaign results?

No. Past views, audience data, estimates, or creator statistics can help inform a decision, but they do not guarantee future views, sales, clicks, conversions, revenue, or other campaign outcomes.

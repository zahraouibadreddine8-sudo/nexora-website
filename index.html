Before we get started

A few things
worth knowing.

How does matching work?

Matching checks approved creator profiles against the campaign’s niche, platform, language, average views, audience country, and creator budget. Nexora reviews profiles, and you decide who to invite.

What does Nexora handle?

Nexora helps coordinate creator discovery, introductions, agreed terms, and campaign progress. Contracts and payments are arranged separately from the platform.

Does signing up guarantee a partnership?

No. Creator profiles are reviewed, brands choose who to invite, and both parties need to agree before any campaign begins.

Can I contact Nexora directly?

Yes. or . These forms reach the Nexora team by email.

Privacy & platform information

Campaign requests and creator applications are sent to Nexora through Web3Forms. Platform accounts use Supabase for authentication and saved records. Your login email is not shown in the creator directory. Approved creator profile details are visible to registered brands; campaign records are visible to their owner, invited creators, and Nexora administrators. The site also uses Vercel Analytics. To ask about your information or request removal, contact Nexora using the campaign request form. Submitting an application or expressing interest does not create a paid agreement. Final deliverables, fees, rights, and payment terms must be agreed separately before work begins.

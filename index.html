A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Before we
get started.

Have a specific campaign in mind?

Who is Nexora for?+

We focus on AI, software, gaming, PC hardware, creator gear, smart home, and related technology brands and creators. The starting point is a relevant product and audience.

Do I need to create an account?+

You can contact Nexora without an account. Create a brand or creator account to save your profile, manage campaigns, and review invitations in your dashboard.

What budget do I need?+

It depends on the creator, format, scope, and content rights. Share a realistic budget—even if it is a rough range—and we can discuss what may fit. We do not promise a fixed number of views or sales.

How does Nexora charge?+

Any Nexora fee is agreed before the campaign starts, alongside the creator’s payment and the scope of work. Sending an enquiry does not commit you to a paid campaign.

How are creators selected?+

We consider content relevance, recent activity and views, audience information, rates, and availability. Creator-provided figures may need supporting analytics before a proposal is finalised.

Will applying guarantee paid work?+

No. Applying lets us assess whether your content fits relevant brand opportunities. Every partnership depends on the brief, budget, availability, and agreement from both sides.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to information you provide to Nexora through this website and the limited website analytics used to understand site traffic.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

ImportantNexora is operated from Morocco. Information submitted through the site may be processed by service providers outside Morocco. Website privacy notices do not replace any registration, notification, authorization, or international-transfer formalities that may be required by applicable law.

1. Who controls your information

Nexora controls the personal information submitted through this website for the purposes described below. Nexora operates from Morocco. To exercise a privacy right or ask a privacy question, use the general contact form and write “Privacy request” at the beginning of your message.

2. Information we collect

SourceInformation
Brand enquiryName, work email, company, optional website, and the campaign/product information you choose to provide.
Creator enquiryName, email, creator/channel link, platform, niche, and any additional message you choose to provide.
General enquiryName, email, and message.
Technical / analytics dataLimited traffic and device/request information used to produce aggregate website analytics and protect the site from abuse.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the website forms unless Nexora specifically asks for it and there is a clear reason to provide it.

3. Why we use the information

  • To read and respond to your enquiry.
  • To evaluate whether a brand, creator, or campaign may be a relevant fit.
  • To follow up on a potential commercial relationship you asked us to discuss.
  • To maintain reasonable business records of partnership conversations.
  • To prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and security problems.
  • To understand aggregate website usage and improve the site.
  • To comply with legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations where they apply.

Submitting a website form does not create a contract or guarantee a partnership.

4. Your choice when submitting a form

Before a form is submitted, you are asked to acknowledge this Privacy Policy and that the submission is processed through Web3Forms so Nexora can respond. You may contact Nexora at any time to ask that your details not be retained for future partnership opportunities after the current enquiry has been handled. That request does not prevent Nexora from keeping information that must be retained for an existing contract, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

5. Service providers and international processing

Web3Forms

The contact forms are transmitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, so the enquiry can be delivered to Nexora. Web3Forms states that form submissions can be stored securely for a configurable retention period of up to three years. See Web3Forms privacy information.

Vercel

The website is hosted on Vercel and uses Vercel Web Analytics. Vercel describes Web Analytics as privacy-focused and designed to work without analytics cookies, while producing aggregate traffic information. See Vercel's Privacy Notice.

Email and business systems

Enquiries may also be received, replied to, and retained in Nexora's email and business record systems. These providers may process information in countries outside Morocco.

Where applicable law requires additional safeguards, notifications, authorizations, or transfer mechanisms for processing outside Morocco, those requirements must be handled separately from this website notice.

6. Cookies and analytics

Nexora's current website code does not use advertising pixels or non-essential marketing cookies. Vercel Web Analytics is currently the only analytics script intentionally included in the site and is designed to operate without analytics cookies. If Nexora later adds advertising, session-replay, or other non-essential tracking technologies, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before those technologies are enabled.

7. Retention

Ordinary website enquiries should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and manage the related business relationship. As an internal operating rule, Nexora aims to review inactive website enquiries after 24 months. Information connected to an actual campaign, payment, contract, dispute, fraud-prevention need, or legal/accounting requirement may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Morocco's Law 09-08 where it applies, you may have rights to access personal information about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, object to certain processing, and request deletion or blocking where the legal conditions are met. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, without affecting processing already carried out lawfully.

To make a request, use the general contact form, start the message with “Privacy request,” and describe what you want Nexora to do. Nexora may need enough information to verify that the request relates to you.

9. Security

Nexora uses reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect website enquiries and limits access to information to people and service providers who need it for the purposes described here. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. No sale of personal information

Nexora does not sell personal information submitted through the contact forms. Information may be shared with service providers used to operate the site, communicate with you, or run an agreed campaign, and where disclosure is required by law.

11. Creator and brand campaign information

If a website enquiry becomes a paid creator partnership, additional information may be needed for contracting, invoicing, payment, shipping, approvals, or campaign delivery. Those details are handled under the applicable campaign documents and any additional privacy information provided at that stage.

12. Changes to this policy

Nexora may update this policy when the website, service providers, business processes, or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the current version.
